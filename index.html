Case 1: Margaret

Outpatient physiotherapy clinic

Patient introduction

Margaret, 68, reports brief episodes of spinning dizziness when rolling over in bed, particularly to the right, and when looking up to reach a shelf. Episodes last around 10–20 seconds and settle when she keeps her head still. She is moving more cautiously in bed and feels anxious about triggering the dizziness.
